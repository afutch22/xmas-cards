The gatefold, start to finish

A working walkthrough of what someone sees after tapping your link. Photos live on the inside of the doors, the drawing is in the middle, and a note waits at the end. Try it in phone view too.

tap the bow

What they experience

The highlighted step follows along as you click through the prototype.

  1. Link previewIn iMessage or email they see your cover image and "tap to open". This comes from the card's preview settings.
  2. The closed cardGreen doors tied with a pink bow, a little snow. One instruction: tap the bow.
  3. Untie and openThe bow slips off, the ribbon falls, the doors swing outward. The card steps back so all three panels fit.
  4. The drawing inks inYour tree draws itself in the center while photos pop onto both doors one by one.
  5. Look at photosTap any photo to see it big with its caption. Swipe or use the arrows to go through all of them.
  6. Read the noteA small tag says "a note for you". Tap it for your handwritten letter.

What you'd prepare

  • 1 drawing for the center (lines plus a color layer)
  • A pattern or drawing for the outside of the doors
  • 4 photos (2 per door), up to 8 with smaller frames
  • A short caption for each photo
  • The note, about 60 to 120 words

On phones

Open flat, the three panels are wide, so on a phone they shrink to fit. The photos are still easy to tap, and the viewer shows them full size. If that feels too small, the other option is that the doors open and then you swipe through three full-screen panels.
